Ledger Arcade Privacy Policy

Effective: November 15, 2026

Ledger Arcade (the "app") collects only the information it needs to run. This policy explains what we collect, why, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

1. Information we collect

ItemWhat it isWhen
Account informationEmail address, only if you choose to sign in with Google, Apple or emailAt sign-in (optional)
NicknameA nickname you type or one the app suggestsFirst launch and when you change it
Learning dataGame scores and ranks, results by level, missed questions (Review Notes) and theory quiz results by topic, saved with your nicknameWhen a game or quiz ends
Usage statisticsWhether the app was opened each day and how many games were started, plus totals such as screen views and ad views, kept with a random device number made inside the app. They contain no name, email address or advertising identifier.While you use the app
Anonymous usage logThe flow of use (app open and close, screen changes, game start and end, ads shown and watched, time, device type and app version), kept with a random anonymous number. This number is not linked to your account, email or nickname.While you use the app
Advertising identifier and install sourceYour device's advertising identifier and similar data used to show ads (on iOS, only if you allow it), and the install referrer that tells us which link led to the installWhen ads are shown or the app is installed

We do not collect your real name, phone number, address or payment information. The app has no in-app purchases.

2. How we use information

  • Keeping you signed in and syncing your progress across devices
  • Providing the leaderboard, progress chart and Review Notes
  • Understanding how the app is used and improving it, using totals that do not identify you
  • Showing ads in the app

3. Services we use

The app uses the following services. Your information may be stored on servers outside your country, including in the United States and the Republic of Korea.

  • Firebase Authentication and Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC, United States) — sign-in and anonymous usage analysis
  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.) — storing nicknames, learning data and usage statistics
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC, United States) — showing ads

Each provider handles information under its own privacy policy (Google: policies.google.com/privacy, Supabase: supabase.com/privacy).

4. Leaderboard

The leaderboard shows only your nickname and score. Your email address and other information are never shown.

5. How long we keep information

We keep information while the service is provided. When you delete your account, the learning data linked to your nickname is deleted with it. The anonymous usage log is deleted after 13 months. Usage totals that cannot identify a person are kept while the service runs.

6. Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: open the app, then Settings, then Delete Account. This cannot be undone.
  • By email: write to the address below and we will delete your data after verifying the request. If you are not signed in, tell us your nickname.

7. Children

The app is intended for people 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If we learn that we hold personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, including under state privacy laws such as California's. We do not sell your personal information. You can limit ad personalization in your device settings. Send requests to the address below.
