Ledger Arcade Privacy Policy

Effective: November 17, 2026

Ledger Arcade (the "app") collects only the information it needs to run. This policy explains what we collect, why, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

2. How we use information

  • Keeping you signed in and syncing your progress across devices
  • Providing the leaderboard, progress chart and Review Notes
  • Understanding how the app is used and improving it, using totals that do not identify you
  • Showing ads in the app

How to refuse, and what happens if you do:

  • If you do not sign in, nothing is sent to Firebase Authentication, but you cannot carry your progress to another device.
  • If you do not want your nickname and learning data stored on Supabase, you cannot use the leaderboard, progress chart or Review Notes. You can ask us to delete them at any time (see section 6).
  • If you decline tracking (iOS) or opt out of personalized ads (Android), you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

Each provider handles information under its own privacy policy (Google: policies.google.com/privacy, Supabase: supabase.com/privacy, Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy, jsDelivr: jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy).

4. Leaderboard

The leaderboard shows only your nickname and score. Your email address and other information are never shown.

5. How long we keep information

We keep information while the service is provided. When you delete your account, the learning data linked to your nickname is deleted with it. The anonymous usage log is deleted after 13 months on our server (Supabase) and after 14 months in Google Analytics. The install source (Android only) is deleted after 13 months. When you delete your account, your nickname is also removed from the daily open records. Usage totals that cannot identify a person are kept while the service runs.

6. Deleting your account and data

  • In the app (if you signed in with Google or Apple): open the app, then Settings, then Delete Account. This cannot be undone.
  • By email: write to the address below and we will delete your data after verifying the request. If you are not signed in, tell us your nickname.

7. Data breaches

If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify affected users and the relevant authority as required by law.

8. Children

The app is intended for people 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If we learn that we hold personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly. If you are under the age of majority where you live, please use the app with a parent's or guardian's permission.

9. Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, including under state privacy laws such as California's. We do not sell your personal information for money. Google AdMob may use your advertising identifier to show personalized ads. You can turn this off in your device settings: on iOS, by not allowing tracking; on Android, in your device settings (Google or Privacy → Ads), you can opt out of personalized ads or delete your advertising ID. Send requests to the address below.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the privacy regulator where you live, for example:

  • Canada — Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca)
  • Australia — Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au)
  • New Zealand — Office of the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz)
