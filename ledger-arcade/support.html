About the app

Ledger Arcade is a study app for Accounting 101. Five mini games and a 150-question theory quiz help you practice accounts, debits and credits, journal entries, adjusting entries and choosing the right books. All questions are original. Every feature is free (the app shows ads).

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to sign in?
No. A nickname is enough to start. Signing in with Google or Apple lets you keep your progress when you change devices.

How do I change my nickname?
Tap the pencil icon next to your nickname on the home screen.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings, then Delete Account. You can also email us (below).

Is this an official exam prep app?
No. It is an independent study aid with original practice questions.
